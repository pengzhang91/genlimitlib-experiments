## How to read the status

- **CC BY 4.0 source:** the linked arXiv edition's license link resolves to
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), or the linked PMLR
  publication is covered by its
  [publication agreement](https://proceedings.mlr.press/pmlr-license-agreement.html).
  Preserve source attribution, license links, and modification notices.
  This does not relicense separately credited material within the paper.
- **Unresolved:** the checked source uses
  [arXiv's non-exclusive distribution license](https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/license.html).
  That license grants distribution rights to arXiv; it is not a general reuse
  license for this repository. No separate permission is recorded here.
  A different published edition may have different terms, but its coverage of
  the actual retained excerpts must be checked before relying on it.

The source links for P02, P04, P06, P08, P09, P12, P17, P19, P23, and P28
follow `sources/01_initial_lean_snapshot/manifest.json`. Links for the remaining
IDs identify the checked paper/edition from the library and bibliography;
they do not establish the exact edition of every excerpt. The historical
manifest records PDF/registry hash mismatches for P04, P17, and P19; those
records remain unchanged. Exact excerpt-to-edition matching remains outside
this source-license review.

## Extraction and modification notice

The artifact stores excerpts as extracted text rather than original page
facsimiles. Extraction can change line breaks, spacing, ligatures, and formula
layout. Excerpts are selected or truncated and assembled with experiment
instructions, answer options, and Lean context. Review-time redactions replaced
some author-identity and contact strings; see [ANONYMIZATION.md](ANONYMIZATION.md).
The original review snapshot remains separate. This public derivative replaces
selected excerpts with exact boundary snippets, source links, and hashes;
PUBLIC_RELEASE.json lists every affected block and question, including
abbreviated supporting quotations in basis fields and the numerical reference. The original
experiment used the complete excerpts. Statements and generated answers in the experiment are not endorsed by
the authors of the source papers.

## Resolving the outstanding entries

For each unresolved entry, retain a record of the permission or other reuse
basis, its scope, the covered source version/passages, and any required notices.
If relying on a conference or journal edition's open license, confirm that the
retained text is covered by that license. Keep this record separate from the
frozen experimental files. Until then, do not describe the complete prompt and
question bundle as fully cleared for redistribution.
