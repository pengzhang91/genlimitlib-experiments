## Material with separate rights

- The copied library under `sources/02_expanded_lean_library/` retains its
  existing [Apache-2.0 license](sources/02_expanded_lean_library/LICENSE).
- Third-party research excerpts in question-bank `paper_text` fields and
  frozen prompts remain subject to their source licenses or other applicable
  permissions. They are not relicensed by the authors' CC BY 4.0 grant.
- The same exclusion applies to third-party text reproduced in annotations,
  model responses, or other mixed records. The grant covers only rights held
  by the project authors.

## Source review

[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) identifies the 16 paper IDs
associated with the frozen question bank, provides attribution and source
links, and records the license evidence checked on 2026-09-28. Six have a
CC BY 4.0 source-license basis; ten checked arXiv sources carry the arXiv
non-exclusive distribution license, which does not itself grant this project
redistribution rights. This is a source-license review, not an excerpt-by-excerpt
clearance of all embedded text or confirmation that each extracted passage
matches the checked edition.

For unresolved excerpts, a rights-holder permission, a matching permissively
licensed edition, or another applicable legal basis still needs to be
documented before treating them as cleared for public redistribution. Merely
crediting a paper or making it freely accessible does not resolve this gap.
This release does not assert that the complete question/prompt bundle has
been cleared.

## This public distribution

The full review snapshot is retained separately by the authors. This public
copy replaces 414 distinct blocks associated with unresolved sources with
short boundary snippets, links, and original hashes. Shared blocks are replaced
wherever reused; mixed-source blocks are abbreviated as a whole when internal
source boundaries are unavailable. Long supporting quotations duplicated in the basis fields and historical
numerical reference are also abbreviated. These decisions are logged in
[PUBLIC_RELEASE.json](PUBLIC_RELEASE.json).

The boundary snippets remain third-party text; shortening is not a declaration
of public-domain status or a new permission grant. The project licenses do not
cover those snippets. An additional formal-edition review is documented in
[the source notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md); it did not establish full coverage
of the exact historical excerpts. The public reproduction command continues
to reconstruct all reported statistics from unchanged response records.
