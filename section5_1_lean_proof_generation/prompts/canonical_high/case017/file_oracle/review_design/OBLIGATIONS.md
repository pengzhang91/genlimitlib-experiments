These items characterize partial progress, not a prescribed proof route.
The reviewer, not the author, records PROVED / PARTIAL / BLOCKED / UNCERTAIN
with exact supporting declarations, established assumptions, and the first
substantive gap. A compiling conditional statement does not construct its
hypotheses. Recognize alternative routes explicitly. Do not rank by a raw
count of covered items; describe the checked dependency chain and distinguish
missing core construction from interface work or final technical cleanup.
