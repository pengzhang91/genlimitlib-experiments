\section{Theoretical Findings}
\label{sec:our-results}
We present three mathematical case studies developed while building and working
with GenLimitLib: a proof repair, a characterization of proper generation
under replay, and an exact mistake--convergence tradeoff for a special family.
The appendix gives the research workflow and complete written proofs; the
accompanying repository contains their Lean verification.
