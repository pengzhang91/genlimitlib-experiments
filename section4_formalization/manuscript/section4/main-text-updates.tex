We present three mathematical case studies developed while building and working
with GenLimitLib: a proof repair, a characterization of proper generation
under replay, and an exact mistake--convergence tradeoff for a special family.
Appendix~\ref{app:proof-status} describes the research workflow and gives

\item \textbf{Mathematical findings from working across the library.}
We repair a gap in a published non-uniform generation proof, preserving its
algorithm and convergence bound. We transfer a three-language construction
to replay, improve a four-language impossibility example to three, and
characterize exactly which finite families permit deterministic proper
generation under replay. For the staircase family, we determine the complete
Pareto frontier of mistake and convergence guarantees. The appendix gives
the written proofs, with accompanying Lean verification in the repository.
